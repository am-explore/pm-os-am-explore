## What This Skill Does
Runs deep competitive research, synthesizes intelligence into actionable positioning decisions, and generates sales-ready battlecards. Goes beyond feature matrices to understand WHY customers choose competitors.

---

## Competitive Research Philosophy

**Most competitive analysis is theater.** PMs look at pricing pages and feature matrices and call it intelligence.

**Real competitive intelligence answers:**
- Why do users choose them over us?
- Why do users choose us over them?
- What are they investing in that we haven't noticed?
- What customers have they decided NOT to serve?

**The counterintuitive insight:** The most valuable competitive intelligence often tells you where NOT to compete, not where to fight harder.

## The 5-Layer Competitive Research Stack

### Layer 1: Public Intelligence (What's Visible)
**Sources:**
- Their website: value prop, pricing, feature list, customer stories
- Product tours / demos: user experience, UX quality, onboarding flow
- G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot reviews: real user pain points and praise
- Job postings: investment areas they're not announcing (15 iOS engineers = mobile push)
- LinkedIn: company growth rate, leadership moves, content themes
- Press releases / blog: roadmap signals, partnership moves
- AppSumo / Product Hunt: early traction data, user feedback patterns

### Layer 2: Customer Intelligence (What's Real)
**Sources:**
- Win/loss interviews: ask every churned user where they went and why
- G2 review text analysis: cluster common praise and complaints
- Sales call recordings: what objections come up? What comparisons?
- Customer success calls: when do customers mention alternatives?
- Support tickets: are users asking for features that competitors have?

**Win/Loss Interview Questions:**
1. "When you were evaluating solutions, who were the other finalists?"
2. "What was the #1 reason you chose [US / THEM]?"
3. "What almost made you choose the other option?"
4. "What would [COMPETITOR] have to change for you to switch?"
5. "What does [COMPETITOR] do better than us? Be honest — it helps us improve."

### Layer 3: Product Intelligence (How They Build)
**Sources:**
- Changelog / release notes: velocity, focus areas
- API documentation: technical capabilities and ecosystem
- Integration ecosystem: what tools they connect to (signals their ICP)
- Mobile app reviews: real usage patterns, bugs, feature requests
- Browser extension downloads / ratings: adoption signal

### Layer 4: Market Intelligence (The Broader Landscape)
**Sources:**
- Analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester, IDC) — where available
- Industry conference talk titles: what problems is the market discussing?
- LinkedIn thought leader content: what narratives are gaining traction?
- Funding news: who is the market backing? With how much?
- M&A activity: consolidation signals, capability gaps being acquired

### Layer 5: Forward Intelligence (What They'll Do Next)
**Signals of future moves:**
- Recent funding + "investing in X" language in press release
- Leadership hires (new VP of Partnerships = ecosystem push)
- Conference keynote themes = strategic priorities
- Patent filings (for larger companies)
- Beta program announcements
