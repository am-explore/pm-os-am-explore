---
name: panel
description: Get a vendor-independent blind review of a consequential document. Use for a second opinion or cross-check on a PRD, trade-off evaluation or strategy doc before committing, when a single model's view isn't enough.
---

Read and apply the full protocol from `skills/execution/SKILL-cross-model-panel.md`.

Use the prompts and record in `templates/panel-review.md`.

**Step 0 first: the privacy gate.** The artifact will be sent to other companies' models. Do not panel anything confidential or sensitive; sanitize it or skip the panel. Use at least two different vendors, identical prompts, fresh sessions, no cross-visibility in round 1. Group results by finding, mark contested ones, and verify every finding against the document yourself. Findings are candidates, not verdicts.
